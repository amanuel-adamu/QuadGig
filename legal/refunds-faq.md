# Refunds & Cancellations — How It Actually Works

## Can I cancel an order?
Yes — either the buyer or the seller can cancel, as long as the order hasn't been marked "confirmed" yet.

## If I cancel, do I get my money back?
Yes, automatically and in full, if payment had already been collected. No fee is deducted from your refund.

## What if the order's already been confirmed?
Once a buyer confirms an order, the seller gets paid and the transaction is final. There's no refund through the platform after that point — this is why it's worth only confirming once you're actually satisfied.

## What if something's wrong before I confirm?
You can flag the order as "disputed" instead of confirming it right away. From there, the buyer can still confirm it later, or either side can cancel it (which refunds the buyer). Right now, Quadgig doesn't have a formal mediation process — you and the other person are expected to work it out directly first.

## Do I still get charged the platform fee if I cancel?
No. Quadgig's 10% fee only comes out of a seller's payout when an order is confirmed and funds are actually released. If an order is cancelled before that point, nothing is kept by the platform — the buyer gets everything back.

## How long does a refund actually take to show up?
Once triggered, it's usually 5-10 business days for the money to actually appear back on your card or bank account — that's standard for how Stripe (and card networks generally) process refunds, not something Quadgig can speed up.

## I'm a seller — can I cancel an order I accepted?
Yes, any time before the buyer confirms it. The buyer gets refunded automatically if they'd already paid.
